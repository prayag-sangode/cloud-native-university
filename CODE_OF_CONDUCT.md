## Our Commitment

Cloud Native University is a free, open learning community built to help engineers grow in Cloud, DevOps, and Cloud Native technologies.

We are committed to creating a **safe, inclusive, respectful, and collaborative environment** for everyone.

---

# Expected Behavior

All participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and professional
- Be welcoming to newcomers
- Use inclusive and clear language
- Respect different viewpoints and experience levels
- Focus on constructive feedback
- Help others learn and grow

---

# Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not allowed:

- Harassment or discriminatory language
- Personal attacks or insults
- Toxic or disrespectful communication
- Spamming or self-promotion without context
- Sharing misleading or harmful technical information
- Disrupting learning or collaboration

---

# Learning Environment Principles

We believe:

- Everyone starts somewhere
- Questions are encouraged
- Mistakes are part of learning
- Teaching others strengthens the community

---

# Collaboration Guidelines

When contributing:

- Be patient with beginners
- Explain “why”, not just “what”
- Keep feedback constructive
- Respect existing structure and design decisions
- Focus on improving learning experience

---

# Reporting Issues

If you experience or observe any inappropriate behavior:

- Open a GitHub Issue (if applicable)
- Or contact the maintainer directly

All reports will be handled **confidentially and fairly**.
